# The owner's standard for every film (26 Nov 2026)

Every mission and every worker builds toward this. It sits beside `docs/TRANSFORMATION.md` §4 (the
measured gates) and `docs/research/VIRAL_STUDY.md` (what the top videos do).

## 1. Story that holds the viewer
- A powerful hook and a cinematic opening: the first 30–60 s must capture attention.
- Intriguing questions, curiosity building, strategic reveals and suspense, emotional engagement.
- Strong narrative progression, with smooth transitions between sections.
- No generic introductions, needless explanation, repeated sentences or boring openings.

## 2. Exceptional visual quality (one of the highest priorities)
- Every scene shows what the narration says and what the topic is.
- Use intelligent selection: high-resolution photography, cinematic archival imagery and relevant
  footage. Use historical photographs where right; maps, diagrams and infographics.
- AI-generated assets only where appropriate and supported.
- Keep framing and composition clean, with one consistent visual identity.
- Never fill a scene with an unrelated picture.
- Never present fabricated imagery as authentic historical evidence.

## 3. Cinematic motion, never a slideshow
- Slow zoom, push-in and pull-out, Ken Burns, controlled pans, parallax depth.
- Layer animation and subject-focused moves; animated maps, routes and timelines.
- Documentary infographics, professional text animation, smooth transitions.
- Motion is intentional and relevant. Never repeat the same zoom; no excess effects.

## 4. Editing and scene composition
- An intelligent editing engine: dynamic shot choice and the right duration per shot.
- Wide, medium and close-up variety, with establishing shots.

## How the Boss reads any instruction (the owner's rule)
An instruction, even the owner's own prompt, is checked before it is followed. Ask whether it fits this
platform and the goal (the world's #1 documentary platform), and whether it is correct. If it has a mistake,
correct it and do better than asked; never follow a wrong prompt word for word.
