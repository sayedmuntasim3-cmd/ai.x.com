# Owner's direction for the Quran recitation documentary (13 Nov 2026)

Part 1 is the owner's own notes, given in Bangla, rendered here in English (a rendering, not his
words). Part 2 is the English direction he sent, verbatim. Where they differ, Part 1 is later and his own.

## Part 1: the owner's notes (rendered from Bangla)

- The recitations he gives need no Bangla or English meaning on screen. The recitation plays, and
  documentary pictures go with it. (The test sample he heard had a spoken translation in it; that is
  not what he wants.)
- Do what is best for YouTube: reach, views, earnings. He does not ask that his word be followed
  line by line; he asks for whatever works best, thought through.
- He wants these recitation films to reach Islamic and Muslim-majority countries most of all.
- An ordinary voice should come out so good that the person who gave it is astonished.
- He will send a recitation of his own later, as a sample of what the platform can make. Until then, test.
- The platform should stay in demand for decades: people come, give a voice (their own recording,
  or one from ElevenLabs or anywhere), and get a film made on that voice.
- Reference for the studio page: kutly.io and his screenshots. Two parts: (1) Idea: an idea becomes a
  script and a film; (2) Voiceover: a voice is given and the documentary is made to fit that voice.
  For a recitation, the pictures and clips are the ones that belong to what is recited; for any
  other topic, the style of that voiceover is understood and matched. The voice system is the main aim.
- He asks for better than this direction, not merely what it says.

## Part 2: the English direction (verbatim)

Project Direction: World-Class Qur’an Recitation Documentary Production System

Act as a world-class software architect, AI video production engineer, cinematic documentary director, audio engineer, and Qur’anic content safety specialist.

I want to develop a genuinely professional, world-class Qur’an Recitation-to-Documentary Video Production System.

This phase is exclusively focused on Qur’an recitation documentaries. Do not mix the USA English documentary project into this phase.

1. Main Objective

The user should be able to upload a Qur’an recitation recording from virtually any recording environment.

The system should automatically analyze the recitation, identify the relevant Qur’anic passages, understand their verified meanings, plan appropriate visual storytelling, select or generate high-quality footage, and produce a complete cinematic documentary video synchronized with the recitation.

The goal is not to create a basic slideshow.

The goal is to create a sophisticated documentary production system that follows the visual storytelling standards of exceptional international documentary filmmaking while maintaining the highest respect for the Qur’an.

2. Intelligent Qur’an Recognition and Understanding

Build a reliable pipeline that:

- Detects the Arabic recitation.
- Identifies the Surah and Ayah whenever possible.
- Matches recognized words against a verified Qur’anic Arabic text.
- Segments the recording into individual verses and meaningful phrases.
- Calculates accurate timestamps for every verse and phrase.
- Uses trusted Qur’an translations and approved tafsir sources to understand the context.
- Maintains the relationship between the recitation, the Arabic text, translation, and visual scenes.

Important: Never invent an Ayah identification, translation, religious interpretation, or historical claim.

If recognition confidence is insufficient, request the Surah and Ayah information from the user instead of guessing.

Allow optional user-provided Surah, Ayah range, translation language, and other metadata.

3. Intelligent Cinematic Visual Storytelling

This is one of the most important parts of the entire project.

The system must not select random beautiful images simply because they look cinematic.

Every visual decision must be guided by the actual meaning and context of the relevant Ayah.

For example:

- Verses about the creation of the heavens and earth may require breathtaking cinematic footage of mountains, oceans, clouds, stars, the sky, and the natural world.
- Verses about mercy, compassion, forgiveness, and kindness may require meaningful, respectful human-centered documentary imagery.
- Verses about historical nations may require contextually appropriate historical environments, architecture, landscapes, and ruins.
- Verses about reflection, patience, gratitude, and guidance may require carefully selected visual storytelling.
- Verses concerning the Hereafter and unseen matters must be handled symbolically and respectfully, never presented as literal depictions of unseen realities.

The system must understand what a scene communicates before deciding to use it.

It must be able to decide:

1. Whether the scene needs nature, people, architecture, historical environments, or symbolic imagery.
2. Whether a real video clip, a still photograph, or AI-generated footage is most appropriate.
3. What type of camera movement is suitable.
4. How long the visual should remain on screen.
5. When to transition to the next scene.
6. How to maintain a coherent visual story throughout the entire recitation.

4. World-Class Cinematic Quality

I want professional documentary-level visual production.

Build the system around these quality requirements:

- Exceptional visual composition.
- High-resolution footage.
- Realistic and natural-looking imagery.
- Professional cinematic lighting.
- Beautiful and consistent color grading.
- Appropriate depth, atmosphere, and visual perspective.
- Smooth, natural camera movement.
- Carefully designed transitions.
- Excellent visual pacing.
- Consistent visual identity throughout the documentary.
- No repetitive stock footage.
- No cheap slideshow effects.
- No unnecessary flashy transitions.
- No visible AI-generation defects, flickering, distorted faces, unnatural motion, or malformed objects.

The system should intelligently choose between licensed footage, appropriate photographs, and AI-generated video according to the needs of each scene.

Never sacrifice visual relevance for visual beauty.

5. Qur’anic Accuracy and Respect

This project requires stricter standards than ordinary entertainment video production.

Mandatory safeguards:

- Never depict Allah.
- Never visually portray Prophets or Messengers.
- Never depict angels or claim to show the actual appearance of unseen beings.
- Never present imagined depictions of Paradise, Hell, or the unseen as factual representations.
- Never fabricate historical religious events.
- Never use disrespectful, inappropriate, or misleading human imagery.
- Use symbolic and contextual imagery when direct representation would be religiously inappropriate.

Arabic Qur’anic text must come directly from a verified text source, not from an image-generation model.

Translations must be traceable to a selected reliable translation source.

Include human review for religious accuracy before final publication.

6. Audio Preservation and Synchronization

The original recitation is the central element of the documentary.

Preserve:

- Original pronunciation.
- Tajweed.
- Makhraj.
- Pitch and melody.
- Natural pauses.
- Recitation speed.
- Verse timing.

Do not use pitch correction, speed manipulation, voice conversion, or AI-generated replacement recitation.

If audio enhancement is used, it must be conservative and non-destructive.

Any noise reduction or reverb must preserve the original recitation and be subject to appropriate testing and approval.

The visual timeline must synchronize accurately with the original recitation, including verse boundaries and natural pauses.

The video must not cut or distort the recitation to accommodate the visuals.

7. Intelligent Editing and Production

Build an intelligent editing engine that automatically controls:

- Scene selection.
- Scene duration.
- Camera movement.
- Visual transitions.
- Color grading.
- Visual continuity.
- Arabic verse display.
- Selected translation subtitles.
- Audio synchronization.
- Opening and ending sequences.

The final result should feel like one carefully directed documentary rather than a collection of unrelated clips.

Do not add background music or sound effects by default. The recitation must remain the focus.

8. Asset Quality and Copyright

Only use footage that is properly licensed or original AI-generated content.

Maintain an asset manifest recording each asset's origin, license, and intended use.

Do not download or reuse other creators' YouTube videos without appropriate rights.

Generated footage must undergo quality checks before being included.

9. User Experience

The ideal workflow should be simple:

1. Upload Qur’an recitation.
2. The system analyzes the audio.
3. The system identifies and verifies the Qur’anic passage.
4. It understands the verified meaning and context.
5. It prepares a complete cinematic visual plan.
6. It selects or generates the appropriate imagery and footage.
7. It edits the entire documentary.
8. It synchronizes visuals, Arabic text, subtitles, and recitation.
9. It performs technical and content-quality checks.
10. It provides a preview and final export.

The user should also be able to review, replace, or approve individual scenes before the final render.

Default output should be professional 16:9 landscape video at a minimum of 1920×1080, with optional 4K and 9:16 formats where supported.

10. Quality Assurance

Do not consider this project successful just because the code executes.

Test the complete pipeline using actual recitation recordings.

Evaluate:

- Qur’anic recognition accuracy.
- Correct Ayah identification.
- Translation accuracy.
- Visual relevance.
- Religious appropriateness.
- Audio synchronization.
- Video rendering quality.
- Performance with different recording qualities.
- Long recording support.
- Final output consistency.

Maintain a distinction between automated technical tests and human evaluation of religious and visual quality.

Never report an untested capability as completed.

11. Implementation Instructions

Before making changes, inspect the existing project, its architecture, current audio engine, video-rendering pipeline, dependencies, and existing features.

Preserve working functionality and existing work.

Do not unnecessarily rebuild or replace existing components.

Identify the missing capabilities, design a coherent architecture, and implement the system in logical stages.

Prioritize a reliable end-to-end working pipeline over superficial features or impressive claims.

Do not stop after writing a proposal. After the audit and implementation plan, proceed with the actual engineering work.

Provide a clear technical report describing:

- What already exists.
- What was improved.
- What was implemented.
- What was actually tested.
- What remains incomplete.
- What requires human approval.

Final Engineering Principle

I do not want an ordinary AI video generator.

I want a highly intelligent, deeply context-aware, technically reliable, cinematic Qur’an Documentary Production System.

Its greatest strength must be the ability to understand the actual Qur’anic content and translate that meaning into respectful, beautiful, meaningful, and professionally directed visual storytelling.

Religious accuracy must always take priority over cinematic effects.

Visual quality must never replace meaning.

The entire system must be designed and engineered to an exceptionally high international documentary production standard.
