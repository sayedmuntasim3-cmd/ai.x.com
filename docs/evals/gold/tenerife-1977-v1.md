## How the sheet is used

- A script statement that **contradicts a high line** is wrong.
- One that contradicts a medium line is flagged and checked by hand.
- One that rests on a fact not in this sheet is checked against its cited
  source; if no source, it is **unsupported**.
- Popular but imprecise statements (T15 "fog", T16 exact words) are marked
  "imprecise", not wrong.
