FAQ

Questions?
Answers.

Everything you need to know about making videos with Zupona AI.

What is Zupona AI?

An AI video studio. You describe a video in plain words, and Zupona researches the topic, writes the script, records a voiceover, finds the footage, adds captions and edits it all together into one finished video.

Do I need any editing skills?

No. If you can write a sentence, you can make a video. You can still change any scene, line or clip whenever you want to.

What kinds of videos can I make?

Documentaries, explainers, YouTube videos, Shorts, Reels, TikToks, lessons, news recaps and brand stories — in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.

Can I post my videos on YouTube and social media?

That's exactly what it's made for. Zupona saves the license and credit for every clip it uses, so you always know where your footage came from and how to credit it.

How do I know the facts are right?

Zupona researches from real sources and links every fact back to where it came from. An AI review team checks the script before you see it — and you can open every source yourself.

Is my work saved?

Yes. Projects save automatically as you work, so you can close the tab and continue later, right where you left off.

How do I get started?

Zupona AI is opening in early access. Tap “Start creating”, type your idea, and you're on your way.
