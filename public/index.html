How it works

From a single sentence to a finished film

  1. 1

    Bring an idea

    One line is enough: a bridge that failed, a dam that should not exist, a night a city flooded.

  2. 2

    Research and script

    Zupona reads the public record, writes the script and keeps a source page for every claim it makes.

  3. 3

    Film with real footage

    Real archive photographs and film run under the narration, with the licence and the source page in the end titles.
