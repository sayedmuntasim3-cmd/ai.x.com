## S4: Production bibles

### S4-0. How to read this stage, and what overrides the engine

- **Every token here is a selection-and-finish spec, not an AI prompt.** The AI reconstruction cap is 0 s (production rule 2), so no AI prompt exists. Each token's **consistency line** (in English) is pasted verbatim into every S5 shot line that uses it. It tells the editor what to pick from Pexels, Pixabay, Videvo (Free license), Coverr (downloaded by hand) or Mixkit (Free License), and how to finish it.
- **Overrides of engine section 8** (the production rules win):
  1. Grain goes on everything, archive or not, with one grain setting for the whole film (rule 5). The engine's "grain on genuine archive only" does not apply, because this film has no archive.
  2. Audio comes only from Pixabay Music, Pixabay sound effects, Mixkit free music and Mixkit free SFX. The engine's Freesound, SFX-library and generator routes are off. No AI-generated audio of any kind.
  3. **Narration is a human recording.** Rule 2 bans AI-generated audio, so a TTS voice is used only as a **scratch track for timing** and never reaches the release (8.1).
  4. The master runs at 23.976 fps, not the engine's 25 fps, for cinema motion cadence (8.4-C).
  5. There is no face reference pack and no face description for any real person. No face of a real person ever appears in this film, and no stock face stands in for one.
- **New token:** LOOK-D (the cold drift, 0:33:00–0:36:50), which S3's C5 brief describes but did not name. LOOK-G is the finishing pass for graphics.
- **Correction to S3:** the plan for SC0070 says "HERO-RAZOR, overhead, with the strop." One clip cannot change its camera angle. HERO-RAZOR is therefore picked as a **set**: two or more clips from the same contributor and the same shoot, one of them overhead (OBJ-01). If no such set exists, SC0070 uses a different-region L5 crop of the hero clip, and S5 drops the word "overhead."

### 8.1 Narrator: SND-VO

| Field | Spec |
|---|---|

| Persona | A calm, curious, observational guide who has read every source and trusts the facts to carry the drama. Direct, personal American documentary storytelling: short declarative sentences, concrete nouns, momentum from "but" and "therefore." |
| Pace | **150 wpm average (2.5 words/s)**, matching S3: 6,210 words over 2,484 narrated seconds. By section: hook 150–158 wpm; information beats 150–155; grief and emotional holds (C1 RS01, the C5 cliff, the C6 payoff) 135–140 with longer pauses. **Number-reading rules (the TTS script and the human script spell every number as spoken words; the screen shows numerals):**
- **Years:** 1989 → "nineteen eighty-nine"; 1993–95 → "nineteen ninety-three to ninety-five"; 2004 → "two thousand four"; 2011 → "twenty eleven"; 2025 → "twenty twenty-five".
- **Dollars lead:** "about [X] dollars," with no "and" inside numbers (American style: "seven hundred fifty").
- **Rupees:** spoken only where S3 writes them. "Five rupees" (SC0030); "three to three point three crore rupees" (SC0192).
- **Crore:** the **first spoken "crore" (SC0192)** adds once: "a crore is ten million rupees." This is arithmetic, not a claim. The words "lakh" and "crore" are never spoken without that gloss having come first.
- **Ranges:** "five hundred to seven hundred fifty haircuts."
- **Ages:** "seven," "about thirteen." His father's death never gets a year.
- **Never spoken:** the CIN code, "Pvt Ltd" (say "Private Limited" only if it must be said), "Rs" (always "rupees"), claim IDs, "[C##]" placeholders.

**Release recording (the human narrator):**
- **Rights:** an original recording the production owns. The narrator is the producer, or a voice actor who signs a recording release: worldwide, perpetual, all media, edits and dubbing allowed, and no cloning or AI training on the voice without separate written consent.
- **Casting:** no celebrity sound-alike, and no voice that resembles Ramesh Babu or any real person in the story.
- **Chain:** large-diaphragm condenser or broadcast dynamic microphone, 15–20 cm from the mouth, 15° off-axis, pop filter. Treated room (RT60 under 0.3 s, noise floor under −60 dBFS). Record 48 kHz/24-bit mono WAV, peaks around −6 dBFS.
- **Processing:** HPF at 70 Hz; −2 dB at 300 Hz; +1.5 dB at 4 kHz (wide); de-ess at 6–8 kHz; compression 2.5:1, 3–6 dB of gain reduction; no reverb (dry and close). Light de-noise only if needed.
- **Delivery:** one WAV per chapter (C1–C6) plus pickups, slated with SC ranges.

**Scratch TTS (for timing the edit and the animatic only; replaced before release):**
- **Voice:** any stock voice in the TTS tool that fits the spec above: adult male, General American, low-mid pitch. Never a cloned or custom voice of a real person.
- **Settings:** speed 1.00, adjusted in 0.02 steps until the calibration passage (SC0011–SC0025, 375 words) measures 150 ± 3 wpm; stability 0.50–0.60; similarity/clarity 0.75; style 0.10–0.20; speaker boost on.
- **SSML pauses:** sentence end 350 ms; beat change 700 ms; before a reveal 1,000 ms (the edit then sets the exact silence, 8.5-D).
- **Lock:** the release VO is recorded against the locked cut. Any scene where the human read drifts more than ±0.5 s from the scratch is re-timed in the edit, not re-paced.

### 8.4 LOOK: the master grade and finish

#### A. Picture format
- **Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 letterbox inside a 3840×2160 master. The active picture is 3840×1608 (from y = 276 to y = 1884), with black bars of 276 px top and bottom.
- **How the bars are built:** as a **matte on the top video track** (two black 3840×276 plates), not as output blanking. That way source chips and labels can sit in the lower bar.
- **Shorts and the Facebook cut:** rebuilt 9:16 or 4:5 from the full-frame sources, with no letterbox and re-set type (S3 G).
- **Color:** SDR Rec.709, gamma 2.4.

#### B. Lens classes (stock is picked by its framing)
- **WIDE** (24 mm feel): establishing shots only. 1080p sources are allowed only here or as half of a split.
- **MID** (50 mm feel): observational.
- **DETAIL** (85 mm and macro): the film's main register, because details hide stock origins best.
- **Rule:** never two identical shot sizes in a row, except a planned match cut.

#### C. Frame rate and conform
- **Master:** 23.976 fps.
- **23.976 or 24 fps sources:** used natively.
- **25 fps sources:** conformed to 23.976 (a 4% slow-down, invisible on B-roll).
- **29.97 or 30 fps sources:** conformed to 80% speed.
- **48–60 fps sources:** used for slow motion at 40–50%.
- **Retiming:** never frame blending. Use "Nearest," and optical flow (enhanced, better) only inside speed ramps.
- **Stock audio:** every stock clip is **muted** (8.5).

#### D. LOOK-M, the master grade (DaVinci Resolve; Studio features have a free-version fallback in brackets)

**Project setup:**
- DaVinci YRGB Color Managed.
- Timeline space: DaVinci Wide Gamut / DaVinci Intermediate.
- Output: Rec.709 gamma 2.4.
- Default input: Rec.709 gamma 2.4, overridden per clip. Use the camera log transform for log drone footage, and Rec.2100 HLG with tone mapping for HLG phone footage.

**Clip-level nodes (one stack per clip):**
1. **N1 Input:** a CST override if the clip is not Rec.709.
2. **N2 Noise reduction:** temporal, 2 frames, better, motion medium; luma/chroma 4/6 on UHD sources, 8/10 on 1080p sources. Spatial NR at 3–5 only if blocks show. [Free version: skip, and lean on N5's blur.]
3. **N3 Balance:** Offset for white balance, until the RGB parade aligns on a neutral surface. Mid-tones sit at about 40–45 IRE.
4. **N4 Privacy:** tracked blur on plates, legible signage, any stray face and third-party logos. Gaussian, strong enough that nothing can be read.
5. **N5 Stock-tell kill:**
   - Hue vs Sat: blues −25%, cyans −20%, greens −15%.
   - Sat vs Sat: compress input above 0.7 by 20%.
   - Lum vs Sat: −30% above 80% luma, −40% below 10% luma.
   - Blur radius 0.48 (0.50 is neutral) on 4K clips with in-camera edge sharpening. Not on 5K or 8K sources.
6. **N6 Chapter trim:** LOOK-A, B, C or D (below).

**Group post-clip "FOOTAGE" (LOOK-M):**
1. **Contrast:** 1.10 at pivot 0.40 (DI). Custom curve toe lifted to 0.03 for a soft black (blacks land at 2–4 IRE). Shoulder rolls off from 0.85 to 0.95, so speculars never clip hard.
2. **Split tone:** shadows (Lift) toward hue 200°, amount 0.04; highlights (Gain) toward hue 45°, amount 0.03; mid-tones neutral.
3. **Saturation:** 0.88 overall.
4. **Halation:**
   - Studio: the Halation OFX, threshold about 0.80, radius medium, tint red-orange, about 10% mix.
   - Free version: luma key 0.80–1.00 with softness 0.10, blur 0.7, tint hue 15°, Add mix at 10%.
   - It only reads where a highlight meets dark (chrome, bulbs, lane lines). That is intended.
5. **Glow:** threshold 0.85, spread 0.3, gain 0.10. Only in LOOK-C and LOOK-D car shots.
6. **Vignette:** soft oval fitted to 2.39, −0.15 stop at the edge.

**Timeline node (everything, footage and graphics):**
- **Film grain, one setting for the whole film:** 35 mm-style, size 0.65, strength 0.18, softness 0.25, grain saturation 0.15. Grain is set slightly coarse so it survives YouTube's re-encode.
- **Order:** grain is applied after all scaling. **Never sharpen after grain.**

**Sharpening and upscaling:**
- No sharpening in the grade.
- 1080p sources: Super Scale 2× (medium, sharpness 0.3, NR 0.3) [free version: "Sharper" scaling], only for wide shots or a half-frame split (S3).

**Push-in limits:**
- 4K sources: 1.15×.
- 5K sources: 1.30×.
- 8K sources: 1.6×.
- Eased in and out, over the full shot length.

#### F. Color-matching rules
1. **One reference still per chapter** (REF-C1 … REF-C6), taken from its hero shot. Every clip is matched against it with split-screen wipes and checked on scopes, not by eye alone.
2. **Match order:** black level → white balance on a neutral surface → mid-tone exposure → saturation → the key hue (sodium amber, chrome, sky).
3. **Neighbors:** adjacent shots differ by no more than 5 IRE in average luma, unless the difference is a planned cut-to-dark.
4. **Skies:** no saturated blue sky anywhere. Desaturate it to half or frame it out.
5. **Skin on stand-in hands:** sits on the vectorscope skin line, ±5°.
6. **Black paint:** true black at 0–3 IRE, with speculars rolled at 92–95.
7. **Phone-shot clips:** a clip that still looks like a phone after N5 (HDR halos, crushed micro-contrast) is dropped.

#### G. Making free stock unrecognizable
**At selection:**
- Search with 2–4 plain nouns plus one mood word.
- Go past the first two screens of results; on Pixabay, sort by newest.
- Where download counts show, prefer the less-downloaded clip, and never take the top results for generic terms ("barber," "luxury car," "money," "city").
- Reverse-image-search one frame of every hero clip and drop it if it is common in ads or thumbnails (S3 I).
- Reject anything with a watermark, burned-in text, people smiling or looking into the lens, a third-party logo outside the story's brands, or an AI-generated label or synthetic look (rule 2).

**At finishing:**
- Crop away stock's centered compositions; reframe to thirds within the 2.39 extraction.
- Never use a clip's first or last 10 frames (stabilizer warp and fades).
- Mix locked shots with the stock-typical slider moves.
- Retime to 23.976.
- Rebuild all sound (8.5).
- Apply LOOK-M, halation and grain to everything.

**Banned clichés:** money rain, handshakes, spinning globes, a man on a mountaintop, champagne, fanned cash, hooded hackers, glowing brains, and exotic India shorthand (snake charmers, slum panoramas, sitar). These rules also follow the US framing guidance: no exoticism and no poverty porn.

#### H. Motion and edit grammar
- **Push-ins:** within the limits in D. **Lateral drift:** at most 3% of frame width per shot.
- **Locked shots for weight:** the chair, the ledger, the tax bill, the jewelry box.
- **Speed ramps:** on sources of 48 fps or higher only: 100% → 40% over 6 frames, hold, → 100% over 4 frames. No more than 3 flashes per second.
- **J-cuts and L-cuts:** ambience or music leads picture by 0.5–1.5 s at every reset.
- **Holds:** emotional beats hold 1–2 s longer than information beats. Holds run up to 12 s in C2 (S3).
- **Match-cut catalog** (MC-01 and MC-02 come from S3; the rest are available to S5):
  - MC-01: comb teeth → lane lines (SC0002)
  - MC-02: scissors closing → car door closing (SC0004)
  - MC-03: razor glint → chrome-handle glint
  - MC-04: ledger pen stroke → road lane line (C3)
  - MC-05: towel steam → rain on a windshield (from the end of C2 into C3)
  - MC-06: strop pull → seat-belt pull
  - MC-07: ledger page → tax-bill page (C5)
- **FX templates in use:** FX-MAP, FX-PAPER (ledger, registry card, tax bill), FX-KINETIC, FX-SPLIT, FX-COUNTER, FX-SOURCECHIP and NONE. FX-FILM is global grain. FX-RECON and FX-ARCHIVE are not used.

### 8.7 Continuity rules
- **Paste tokens verbatim:** S5 lines carry token IDs, and the consistency lines live here.

- **Era:** C1–C3 objects stay timeless. Present-day markers (metro, glass towers, LED tools, smartphones) are allowed only in C6, and in hook shots that pass the era check in 8.3.
- **Source log:** each clip's site, ID or URL, author, license, download date, resolution, fps, page PDF, uses (SC IDs and timecodes) and L5 variant number (n/3).
- **Reuse:**
  - Any clip returns at least 10:00 after its last use, at most 3 times in the film, and the new use differs by at least 50% of the frame area (S3).
  - No clip appears twice in one chapter.
  - The hero reuse table (S3 I) is binding.
